What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize item links and comparison notes. Individual lists differ in ownership, accuracy and update frequency; a listing alone does not verify quality, authenticity or availability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a browsing guide and direct links to CAFANS categories. It does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or a live inventory feed.

Can a spreadsheet link guarantee product quality or authenticity?

No. A product link or brand name does not establish quality or authenticity. Review seller information, detailed photos, materials, measurements and applicable return terms before buying.

What should I compare before choosing an item?

Compare the exact variant, measurements, materials, detailed photos, seller terms and estimated total cost. Include domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and applicable taxes where relevant.

Is this an official Oopbuy or fashion brand website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS discovery guide. It is not presented as an official Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website.
